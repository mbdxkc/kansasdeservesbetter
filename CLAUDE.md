## Why this site exists

Google Ads rejects profanity in ad text and display URLs, and requires the shown domain to match the landing page. This page is the destination for paid ads. It carries real content and links to fuckrogermarshall.com with an ordinary button. **Never add an automatic redirect** (meta refresh, JS or server): a page that forwards visitors to another domain is a bridge page and gets ads disapproved.
