Roger Marshall: quick answers

Did Roger Marshall sue his patients?

Yes. Court records show Marshall and his Great Bend OB-GYN practice sued more than 700 patients over unpaid bills from 1998 to 2019, some for as little as $101, at 18 percent interest.

Source: The New York Times, from Kansas court records

Were Roger Marshall's patients arrested?

In 81 of those cases, the patient was arrested after missing a court date. Marshall's spokesperson says judges, not doctors, issue warrants.

Source: The New York Times; KAKE News

How has Roger Marshall voted on health care?

He voted for the 2025 megabill and its Medicaid cuts, against a $35 insulin cap for privately insured patients in 2022 and against extending ACA premium tax credits in 2025.

Source: Senate roll calls 372, 314 and 644

Who is running against Roger Marshall in 2026?

Democrat Adam Hamilton, a United Methodist pastor. Kansas votes Nov. 3, 2026.

Source: Kansas Reflector

When is the voter registration deadline in Kansas?

Oct. 13, 2026, for the Nov. 3 general election. You can register online with a Kansas driver's license or state ID.

Source: Kansas secretary of state

When does early voting start in Kansas?

In-person advance voting can start Oct. 14 and ends at noon Nov. 2. Days and hours vary by county.

Source: Kansas secretary of state
