Kansas U.S. Senate · Nov. 3, 2026

Hamilton wins!

Adam Hamilton defeated Sen. Roger Marshall and flipped the seat. Kansas elected its first Democrat to the U.S. Senate since 1932.

Sources: Johnson County Post, reporting the Associated Press call; NPR.
